Our Story

Craft, dedication, and culinary discipline.

Founded in 2018, The Golden Fork began as a singular vision: to challenge convention by fusing raw, traditional wood-fire cooking with elevated fine dining execution.

We work directly alongside local bio-farms to guarantee that what lands on your plate has spent less time in transit and more time growing naturally. Our culinary team handles baking, curing, and fermenting strictly in-house.
